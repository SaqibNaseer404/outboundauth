Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026. OutboundAuth is a free toolkit run by Saqib Naseer Khan. This policy explains what data is handled when you use it.

Tool inputs stay in your browser

The SPF, DKIM, DMARC, header, and subject-line tools run entirely in your browser. Records and headers you paste are analyzed locally on your device — they are not sent to, stored on, or logged by any OutboundAuth server.

No accounts

OutboundAuth does not require accounts and does not set its own tracking cookies or analytics identifiers.

Analytics cookies (with your consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — for example, how many people visit each day, how long they stay, and which pages and traffic sources are most useful. Google Analytics sets cookies only after you click "Accept" on the cookie notice. If you click "Decline," no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and opt out via Google's opt-out add-on.

Cookies and third-party advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. If no ads are currently displayed, this section describes what applies once advertising is enabled.

Contact information

If you contact Saqib by email or WhatsApp about services, the information you share (your name, domain, and message) is used only to respond to your inquiry and provide the requested service. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify, which may collect standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) to operate and secure its infrastructure. See Netlify's privacy policy for details.
