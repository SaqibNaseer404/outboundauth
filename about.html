About OutboundAuth

OutboundAuth is a free email deliverability toolkit. It checks your SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup, builds the records for you, and explains what to fix — in plain English.

Why this site exists

Good emails land in spam every day, and most senders never find out why. Authentication records — SPF, DKIM, DMARC — decide whether mailbox providers trust your mail, but they're written in DNS syntax that reads like a foreign language. OutboundAuth exists to close that gap: run a check, get a straight answer, fix the problem.

What you get for free

Everything in the toolkit is free to use, no account needed:

  • Health check — grades your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC in one pass.
  • Record builders — generates correct SPF and DMARC records for your setup.
  • Header analyzer — reads authentication results from real message headers.
  • Guides — plain-English explainers on SPF, DKIM, DMARC, spam causes, warmup and more.
