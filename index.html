Straight answers

Do we need to change our phone number?

No. The workflow is designed to sit behind your existing business number and respond when a call is missed.

Is this a voice bot?

The starting offer is missed-call text-back and booking. It is deliberately simpler and less intrusive than making every caller talk to a robot.

Can a customer reach a real person?

Yes. Your team can take over, and conversations that meet your escalation rules can be routed directly to a human.

Will it promise times we can’t honor?

No. Availability and booking rules are defined with you. The workflow can offer approved windows, request a callback, or hand off instead.

Is the demo above a live production system?

It is an interactive sales simulation of the customer experience. A real deployment is connected and tested with your phone workflow, availability, and business rules before launch.
