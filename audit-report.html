Your deliverability audit report, generated live

Enter a domain and this page runs the same live checks as a paid OutboundAuth audit — SPF, DKIM, DMARC, mail setup and blacklists — then lays them out as a plain-English report you can print or save as a PDF. It is the automatic version of our sample audit.

Private by design. The checks are live lookups of public DNS records, run when you press the button. Nothing about your domain is stored — the report exists only on this page, in your browser.

Generate a report

What the report covers

  • SPF — who is allowed to send as your domain, and whether the record is built safely.
  • DKIM — whether your mail is being signed, checked at the ten most common selectors.
  • DMARC — whether you have a policy that stops impersonation, and whether reports are being collected.
  • Mail setup (MX) — whether the domain can receive mail, and on which servers.
  • Blacklists — whether your sending IPs appear on the seven lists that matter most.

Scores are weighted the same way across OutboundAuth: SPF 30, DKIM 25, DMARC 25, mail setup 10, blacklists 10 — one overall score out of 100, with the top problems listed in the order you should fix them.

Honest limits

Authentication is the foundation of deliverability — it proves your email is really from you. It does not, by itself, guarantee inbox placement. Where your mail lands also depends on your sending reputation, your list quality, and your content. Any service that promises “100% inbox” is not telling you the truth. What this report gives you is a straight answer about your authentication, in plain English, from records anyone can look up.

Results are guidance, not a replacement for testing with your sending provider.
